- One browser drone uses the nearest fresh phone as its controller, with hysteresis and a fresh 0.4-second hold after handoff. Phone compass determines forward/left/right. Release, confidence loss, geometry loss, network loss, and backgrounding stop phone motion.
- Phone flight uses the existing collision-aware controller: climb, descend, stop, orbit the operator, directional movement, 90-degree turn, and return toward the starting point. Movement is 2 m/s; descent 1 m/s; climb capped at 10 m above the starting point. Extra fleet controls and laptop-camera capture are hidden/disabled in this phone workflow.
