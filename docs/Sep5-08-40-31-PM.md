# Tighten camera, fade motion traces, and simplify flight input

**Completed:** September 5, 2026 at 8:40:31 PM EDT (UTC-04:00)

## Prompt

Zoom closer to individual drones, replace permanent route lines with quickly fading motion traces, make WASD relative rather than compass-based, and make single-drone deployment complete with one map click.

## Summary

Updated local flight mode so small fleets are framed much more closely, movement draws a short fading streak, WASD maps to the current screen axes, and single deployment places a normal drone immediately at a safe default height when the operator clicks a visible surface.

## Changes

- Reduced automatic camera minimum range from 85 m to 32 m and tightened multi-drone framing while retaining bounding-sphere coverage.
- Added a camera-axis conversion helper so W/S follow screen depth and A/D follow screen left/right regardless of compass orientation.
- Replaced the visible persistent route and animated route arrows with 18 short trace segments that fade nonlinearly to transparent over 1.3 seconds.
- Preserved complete route points internally for undo and replay even though they are no longer permanently drawn.
- Included manual, mission, group-destination, and replay movement in the fading trace behavior.
- Changed **Deploy** so the next valid map click immediately places one normal drone 20 m above the selected surface.
- Kept the existing confirmation workflow for bulk deployment and multi-drone destination commands, where count, spacing, and height still require review.
- Updated on-screen help and local-mode documentation.

## Notes

- Screen-relative movement uses Cesium's live camera direction and right vectors projected into the drone's local east/north plane.
- Clicking sky or an unavailable surface does not deploy; the dock asks the operator to click a visible surface.
- The fading trace is purely visual. Full route geometry remains available for deterministic replay.
