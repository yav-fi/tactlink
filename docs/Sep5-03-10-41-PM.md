# Survey drones and collision indicators

Completed: 2026-09-05 15:10:41 EDT (America/New_York).

Request: implement the accepted non-weaponized Normal/Survey alternative, with crash indicators on both types.

Added deployment type selection for singles and batches, defaulting to Normal. Survey drones have a colored translucent camera cone attached at its apex and pointing forward. It follows manual heading and movement/playback direction. Both drone types display a four-second collision marker on detected manual, mission or playback blockage. Reset clears indicators and fleet cleanup removes all added entities. Fleet and group lists show drone types.

Verification: all 20 tests and TypeScript/Vite production build passed. New tests check cone apex/heading geometry, dynamic color, bulk typing, cleanup and collision marker/reset for both types. No live rendering validation performed for this change.

The cone illustrates field of view only; it does not detect objects or model occlusion. Crash indicators mark the stopped drone position, not a computed surface-contact point. No damage or explosive behavior was added.
