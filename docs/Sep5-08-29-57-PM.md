# Simplify flight controls and automate camera framing

**Completed:** September 5, 2026 at 8:29:57 PM EDT (UTC-04:00)

## Prompt

Replace the crowded local-flight sidebar with one slightly glassy command surface centered at the bottom, begin with a camera orbit around the Washington Monument, and automatically follow/frame drones while circling subjects that have little motion.

## Summary

Redesigned local mode around a compact bottom command dock and added a cinematic camera loop. The default view now circles the Washington Monument before deployment, follows the fleet's collective center after deployment, continuously adjusts range to retain every drone, and resumes a slow orbit when fleet motion is minimal.

## Changes

- Replaced the always-open local sidebar with a translucent centered dock containing only **Deploy**, the drone selector, **Fly**, and **More**.
- Moved speed, batch, destination, playback, reset, mission JSON, view, and runtime-switching controls into the single closed **More** drawer.
- Made deployment controls appear contextually inside that drawer.
- Hid redundant manual camera buttons and made automatic fleet framing the default view.
- Added smoothed collective-center and range tracking based on a bounding sphere around all live/playback drone positions.
- Added slow monument orbit with an empty fleet and slow fleet orbit after 0.65 seconds of minimal subject motion.
- Preserved first-person view as an advanced drawer option.
- Added a reusable camera-framing helper and a deterministic test proving framing range expands with fleet spread.
- Updated local-mode documentation.
