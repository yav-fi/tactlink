# Stop stray hand shapes and dropouts from cancelling held gestures

**Completed:** 2026-09-06 10:52:08 EDT (America/New_York)

## Prompt

Gesture control is still finnicky.

## Two more faults in the same path

The previous fix (`13cb39c`) covered the case where the classifier reports
nothing while the hand is in frame. Two related paths were still open.

**A single frame of a misread hand shape could cancel a held command.**
`FingerMotionInterpreter.update` emitted `label` the instant `threeFingers` or
`twoFingers` was true for one frame. That label becomes the gesture source in
`gesture-camera.ts`. The source is what `GestureCommandRepeater` matches on, and
a source that changes to *something else* — rather than to nothing — takes the
short 220 ms release path, not the dropout tolerance added last time. So two
stray frames within 220 ms stopped the drone.

Those frames are easy to produce. `deriveFingerMotionInput` reads a projected
2D hand: `straight()` compares `fromWrist(tip) > fromWrist(pip) * 1.02` on x and
y only, so a curled finger on a rotating hand can measure as straight for a
frame or two. A held thumbs-up is exactly that shape.

**Acquiring a gesture restarted on every dropout.** `GestureHoldInterpreter`
needs `GESTURE_HOLD_MS` (400 ms) of one gesture in a row, and tolerated only
`GESTURE_DROPOUT_GRACE_MS` (180 ms, about three frames at 16 fps) of
unclassified frames before resetting `heldSince`. With a flickering classifier
the hold kept starting over, so a pose had to be "found" several times before it
ever commanded anything.

## What changed

- `3d-mapping/src/finger-motion.ts` — a hand shape must hold for
  `LABEL_STABLE_MS` (200 ms) before it is allowed to name the hand. Actions are
  unaffected: `ThreeFingerForward` already required a 450 ms hold and the swing
  detector its full V-H-V-H pattern, so only the label is gated.
- `3d-mapping/src/gesture-hold.ts` — `update` takes `handPresent`. A hand still
  in frame gets `GESTURE_PRESENT_GRACE_MS` (500 ms) of unclassified frames
  before the hold resets, instead of 180 ms. With no hand in frame the original
  short window still applies.
- `3d-mapping/src/gesture-camera.ts` — passes `handPresent` to the hold
  interpreter as well as the repeater, and accepts `?score=` to override the
  classifier confidence threshold without a rebuild.

## Verification

- `tsc --noEmit` + `vite build` clean. `npm test` 82/82 pass, including two new
  tests:
  - "a flickered hand shape cannot take over the gesture source"
  - "a hand in frame accumulates its hold across unclassified frames"
- **Mutation-checked.** Returning the raw shape instead of the steady one fails
  the first; pinning the dropout window to `GESTURE_DROPOUT_GRACE_MS` fails the
  second. All other tests passed under both mutations.
- Measured against the pre-change sources, 10 s of held thumbs-up at 16 fps with

## Notes and limitations

- The noise rates above are modelled, not measured from the real recognizer. The
  mechanisms are read from the code and are real; the rates only set where the
  old behaviour starts breaking down.
- `LABEL_STABLE_MS` costs 200 ms before a genuine two- or three-finger pose is
  recognised as the source. The actions those poses trigger already required
  longer holds, so nothing became harder to trigger.
- The classifier confidence threshold is still 0.5 and is the one knob not
  tuned from evidence. `?score=0.35` makes poses easier to register; the 400 ms
  hold still gates every command, so a brief false positive cannot fire one.

## Follow-up

- `deriveFingerMotionInput` works from projected x/y only. MediaPipe supplies a
  z per landmark, and using it would cut the misreads at the source rather than
  filtering them downstream.
