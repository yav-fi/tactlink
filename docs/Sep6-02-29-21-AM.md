# Continuous gesture and flight rendering polish

Completed: 2026-09-06 02:29:21 AM EDT (-0400)

## Prompt

Animate only the modern drone's propellers, make thumb-up, closed-fist,
three-finger W, and pointing-based motion continue while held and stop when the
pose disappears, start the default drone on the ground beside the Washington
Monument, remove goto jitter, and make the red particle trace denser and finer.

## What changed

- Split the four propeller assemblies into independent GLB nodes and embedded a
  looping six-revolutions-per-second animation with adjacent rotors turning in
  opposite directions. Both local and runtime models enable embedded animation.
- Added a held-gesture repeater with a 220 ms dropout grace. Closed fist turns
  90 degrees, waits about one second, and repeats; Pointing Up orbits only while
  held. Releasing an active momentary pose emits one stop.
- Changed thumb-up and three-finger/two-finger flight from repeated short goto
  missions to continuous accelerated velocity control. Motion stops when its
  pose is lost.
- Ground-sampled the default launch coordinate on the plaza south of the
  Washington Monument and positioned the model so its landing rails rest on
  that surface. It now starts parked rather than automatically orbiting.
- Smoothed the displayed aircraft heading and retained a local avoidance
  waypoint during goto operations to prevent frame-to-frame detour zig-zag.
- Increased the particle pool from 28 to 72, reduced each dot to at most five
  pixels, increased sampling density, and added a small deterministic spatial
  spray around the path while retaining the 2.8-second fade.
- Updated tests and local-mode documentation.
