# Survey distance attenuation

Completed: 2026-09-05 15:34:47 EDT (America/New_York).

Request: weaken the Survey effect beyond 100 m and progressively farther away.

Added opacity multipliers of 1 through 100 m, 0.6 through 250 m, 0.3 through 500 m and 0.12 beyond 500 m. Split cone faces into interpolated distance bands. Coverage triangles use the farthest sampled vertex distance; stored opacity survives group recoloring and undo. Added a UI legend and README explanation.
