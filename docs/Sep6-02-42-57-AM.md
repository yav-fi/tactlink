# Smooth drone motion by clearing flight corridors instead of probing every frame

Completed: 2026-09-06 02:42:57 AM EDT (-0400)

## Prompt

The simulator lags and looks jaggety while the drone climbs, descends, or
travels, but renders fine while the drone is parked; the camera is smooth, so
something in the motion logic or rendering needed to be smarter.

## Cause

`DroneController.resolveMove` called `avoidanceMove` on every animation frame
for every moving drone, and `movementBlocked` answers with
`Cesium.Scene.pickFromRay`. In Cesium 1.129 that call swaps to an offscreen
view, re-runs `updateAndExecuteCommands` over the whole Google 3D tileset, and
then performs two synchronous GPU pixel readbacks (`pickFramebuffer.end` and
`pickDepth.getDepth`). Each one stalls the frame it runs on, so a drone in
motion paid one full extra scene traversal plus two pipeline flushes per frame,
and up to seven while avoiding an obstacle. A parked drone skipped the query
entirely, which is why standing still looked smooth.

Two smaller contributors: `smoothRenderedHeading` blended by a fixed 0.16 per
call rather than by elapsed time, so the turn rate changed with frame rate, and
the 72 motion-trace particles allocated fresh `Color` objects on every frame.

## What changed

- Added `MotionGuard` in `3d-mapping/src/collision.ts`. It probes a straight
  corridor ahead of the drone once, sized to a quarter second of flight
  (4-30 m), and then flies inside that verified corridor for free. The corridor
  is re-probed only when it is used up, when it ages past half a second, or
  when the requested heading changes by more than about 1.8 degrees.
- When the corridor is blocked, the guard searches for a detour at probe range
  and holds that heading for the corridor, instead of choosing a fresh
  deflection angle every frame. This also removes per-frame avoidance zig-zag.
- Split the detour candidate search out of `avoidanceMove` as `detourAround`
  so the guard does not repeat the building query it already ran.
- `DroneController` now owns a guard for live flight and one for replay, clears
  both on every transition that moves the drone or changes its route, and
  threads the frame delta into `resolveMove`.
- Made heading smoothing frame-rate independent (`1 - exp(-9 * dt)`).
- Reused per-particle `Color` instances in the motion trace.

- `npm run build` in `3d-mapping`: TypeScript and Vite production build passed.
- Instrumented the new test: one second of 60 fps straight flight now issues
  **5 building queries instead of 65**, a 13x reduction in the stalling call.
- `git diff --check`: no whitespace errors.

## Notes

- `photorealistic ground can be below the hidden fallback ellipsoid` now
  reverses course to leave the cleared corridor before asserting that a newly
  introduced wall blocks the drone. Its original intent, that the hidden
  fallback globe is never queried and that a building pick still blocks flight,
  is unchanged.
- Obstacle avoidance is now slightly earlier rather than later: the drone
  reacts to something within its corridor instead of within a single 0.45 m
  step, which is both safer and visually calmer.
- Survey drones still fire 13 picks in one frame every 500 ms in
  `updateSurvey`. That is a separate, optional-mode hitch and was left alone to
  keep this change focused on the flying drone.
