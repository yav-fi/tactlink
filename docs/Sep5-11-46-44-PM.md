# Planner, gesture, aircraft, and camera polish

Completed: September 5, 2026 at 11:46:44 PM EDT (UTC-04:00)

## Prompt

Fix the unreachable local planner, improve unreliable gesture recognition
without changing any gesture-to-command mappings, make the drone body visibly
partially black, and ease the automatic camera rotation to a stop when the
drone stops.

## What changed

- `./start` now starts and owns the waypoint-planner bridge on port 8766,
  includes it in readiness checks and cleanup, and reuses an existing healthy
  bridge when one is already running.
- The Cesium planner overlay connects automatically and retries quietly while
  its local bridge is starting.
- Preserved all original hand signals and their commands. Recognition now runs
  at a higher sampling rate, accepts the model's normal 50% confidence floor,
  and tolerates a single brief low-confidence/dropout interval without
  resetting the existing 400 ms hold.
- Rebuilt the UAV with a graphite shell, an unmistakably black raised canopy
  and lower body panels, red top and side streaks, and retained red nose detail.
  Runtime role tinting is reduced so it no longer washes out those materials.
- Replaced perpetual idle camera circling with a quadratic four-second coast
  that reaches exactly zero angular speed.
- Updated the project and mapping documentation for automatic planner startup,
  the default aircraft, and camera behavior.

pointing up orbits, the I-love-you pose returns home, and an open palm halts.
