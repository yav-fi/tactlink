# Tighten collision boundary

Completed: 2026-09-05 15:01:38 EDT (America/New_York).

Request: collisions feel too expanded.

Reduced the collision clearance from 10 m to 5 m, narrowing the ray query width from 20 m to 10 m. This permits closer approaches to buildings and ground across manual movement, batches, missions and replay. The collision footprint is intentionally smaller than the enlarged display prisms. Existing query-error and negative-height fixes remain in place.

Verification: all 18 regression tests and the TypeScript/Vite production build passed. No live proximity test performed for this tuning change.
